Kinetra (AI Smart Gym Platform)

One smart machine, an AI coach, and the software to run the business behind it. Members check in each morning, get a daily health score and train with loads that adapt to how they feel. Your team runs sales, installations, memberships, coaching and the machine fleet from one console.

What it doesFeatures

♥Body status & health score

  • Sleep, recovery, activity, stress and nutrition
  • One 0–100 score compared with recent days
  • One suggested next step each day
  • 30-second morning check-in sets readiness

✦AI coach

  • Knows check-ins, sessions, meals and habits
  • Builds workouts and habits from one sentence
  • Training, recovery and nutrition answers 24/7
  • Runs on a local model: data stays on your server

⚡Smart training

  • 7 machine modes, from Standard to Reactive
  • Loads adapt to readiness and history
  • Rep-by-rep speed, power and left/right balance
  • Classes, programs and a 90-exercise library

◉Live & AI coaching

  • Live classes from home with a real coach
  • Small groups at the studio
  • AI-led sessions any time
  • Booking, reminders and a session room

▦Habits, nutrition & progress

  • Habit heatmaps: every square is a day
  • Meals from a photo or a sentence
  • 5-test AI evaluation and adaptive plans
  • Twelve weeks of progress, plus challenges

▤Operations console

  • Sales pipeline fed by the website
  • Orders and installs, from survey to set-up
  • Billing, memberships and the machine fleet
  • Coaching flags, content, staff and settings
