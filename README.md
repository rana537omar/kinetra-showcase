## Features

### Member app
- **Body status and a daily health score**: sleep, recovery, activity, stress and nutrition from the member's own data, shown around a body figure. They roll into one 0–100 score that is compared with the member's recent days, plus one suggested next step.
- **30-second morning check-in**: sleep, energy, mood, stress and soreness set today's readiness, and every load in today's session adapts to it.
- **AI coach, 24/7**: knows the member's check-ins, sessions, meals and habits. It builds workouts on request, creates habits with reminders, and answers training, recovery and nutrition questions. It runs on a local AI model, so member data stays on your own server.
- **Training**: trainer-led classes in 10 categories (strength, hybrid, Pilates, row, kayak, canoe, paddleboard, SkiCross, swim, boxing), multi-week programs, a 90-exercise library and a freestyle builder.
- **Session player with 7 machine modes**: Standard, Adaptive, Eccentric Overload, Chains, Burnout, Perturbation and Reactive. Loads are recommended from readiness and history, and every rep is measured: load, bar speed, power and left/right balance, with slow reps flagged.
- **5-test AI evaluation**: strength, power, balance and mobility scores that generate a 4-week adaptive plan.
- **Live coaching**: live classes from home with a real coach, small groups at the studio, or AI-led sessions any time, with booking, reminders and a session room.
- **Habits with heatmaps**: goals like 8,000 steps or 2 L of water. Every square is a day, with notes and photos per day.
- **Nutrition, progress and challenges**: log a meal from a photo or a sentence, follow twelve weeks of progress charts, and compete on community leaderboards.

### Operations console
- **Overview**: members, recurring revenue, sessions, average readiness, sales pipeline, orders in fulfilment, revenue, machines and service tickets.
- **Members and coaching**: each member's health score and insight, readiness, evaluations, workouts, nutrition, habits and coach messages, plus coaching flags for pain reports, low readiness and inactivity.
- **Sales pipeline**: website demo requests arrive automatically and move from new to demo booked, proposal and won.
- **Orders and installs**: site survey, shipping, delivery, installation and set-up. Finishing an install creates the machine record and the member account.
- **Billing**: plans, subscriptions and invoices, with memberships in US dollars and machines in the business currency.
- **Live and videos**: schedule live, studio and AI sessions, and upload coach video demos.
- **Machine fleet and service desk**: every machine reports in over the device API, with firmware, owner, warranty and service tickets.
- **Content, staff and settings**: classes, programs, exercises and foods; team roles; brand, currency and time zone.

### Public website
- A brand site with features, coaching formats, machine packages and membership plans.
- Showroom demo booking that feeds the sales pipeline.

### Built to run
- One small server with its own database. The AI coach runs on a local model; no outside AI service is needed.
- Light and dark themes, works on any phone browser, and brand-ready: name, colours, currency and photos.
- Connected machines use the device API. This demo uses simulated machine data.
